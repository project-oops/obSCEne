# D067 - The headline number is a frontier, not a sum

**Status:** decided
**Date:** 2026-09-26

`OBS|frontier` reports capabilities established, checks that never ran because a capability they
needed was not established, and the deepest wholly-green section. It is measured over the
capability graph, not section order.

**Why:** a pass/fail total has no denominator and treats a foundation failure like a leaf one, and
growing the census moves it without changing what a bug would reveal. The count of checks stuck
behind the floor is the number an emulator author can move.

**Rejected:** a headline tally - rewards the wrong work.
